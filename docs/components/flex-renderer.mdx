---
title: "FlexRenderer"
description: "Render deformable flex surfaces and lines"
icon: "cloud"
---

```tsx
<MujocoCanvas config={config}>
  <FlexRenderer />
</MujocoCanvas>
```

Accepts normal R3F group props. Uses `flex_elem` for indexed 2D triangles and 1D
line segments, and `flex_shell` for the external triangles of 3D flexes. Vertex
positions follow `data.flexvert_xpos` each frame and surface normals are updated.
Color and opacity come from `flex_rgba`. Flex textures and physical thickness
visualization are not yet supported. Models without flexes render nothing.
